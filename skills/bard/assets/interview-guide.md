## How to run this

- Ask for specific moments, not opinions. If an answer is general, follow up with "can you walk me through a specific time?"
- Write down exact words when something quotable comes up, and mark whether it's verbatim or paraphrased.
- Follow anything surprising, even if it skips the next question.
- Ask permission before using their name or quotes publicly.

## Questions

**Before**
1. <What was normal for you before ___?>

**What happened**
2. <Walk me through the moment you first noticed ___. Where were you?>
3. <What did you expect would happen?>
4. <What was the hardest point for you?>

**Turning point**
5. <What changed things? What did you see, decide, or say?>

**Results**
6. <What numbers show the before and after? Where could we verify them?>

**Meaning**
7. <What surprised you most?>
8. <What would you want <audience> to understand about this?>
